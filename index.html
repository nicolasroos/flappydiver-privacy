Privacy Policy for Flappy Diver

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Introduction

Nico Scuba / Nicolas Roos ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Flappy Diver mobile application (the "App"). This page explains what information we collect, why, and your choices. You can play Flappy Diver with no account and no personal data collection at all. Some optional features — the global leaderboard and our newsletter — do involve personal data, as described below.

Playing Without an Account

If you just play the game, we do not collect any personal information. The App stores the following data locally on your device only, and it never leaves your device:

  • Game Scores: your best local scores.
  • Settings: your music and sound effect preferences.

Accounts and Global Leaderboard

To appear on the global leaderboard you can choose a diver name, and you can optionally sign in with Apple or Google so your name and scores stay with you across devices. If you use these features, we collect:

  • Account identifier. A unique identifier from Apple or Google. We do not post to your account and we never receive your password.
  • Email address. Received from Apple or Google when you sign in. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, we receive Apple's private relay address instead of your real one — email we send still reaches you through it.
  • Diver name. The public display name you choose for the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name — it is shown publicly.
  • Scores. Your game scores and the deepest zone you reached, shown publicly on the leaderboard next to your diver name.
  • Country. Detected from your device's region setting and editable in your profile. We store the country only — not your precise location, GPS, or address.
  • Newsletter preference. Whether you opted in to our news and updates, and the date you opted in.

We do not collect your real name, contacts, precise location, photos, or device advertising identifiers.

Newsletter and Marketing Emails

We send marketing emails (news and product updates) only if you actively opt in by ticking the newsletter box when you create your diver name or later in your profile. The box is never ticked for you. This is your consent under the GDPR, and we record the date you gave it.

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the newsletter toggle off in your profile, or using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. Withdrawing is free and does not affect your ability to play or use any feature.

Legal Basis (GDPR)

For players in the EU/EEA: we process your account identifier, diver name, scores, email, and country to provide the game and leaderboard you asked for (contract / legitimate interest). We send marketing emails only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data — contact us or use in-app deletion.

How It's Stored

Account data, diver names, scores, email, and country are stored using Supabase (our backend provider) on secure servers, and transmitted over encrypted connections.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from inside the App:

Profile → Delete Account

This permanently deletes your account, email, diver name, country, newsletter preference, and global scores from our servers. Your local high scores stay on your device (you can clear those by deleting the App). Account deletion cannot be undone.

Third-Party Services

The App contains no advertising, does no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and we do not sell or share your data. We use your email only to send you our own newsletter, and only if you opted in. The third parties involved are:

  • Apple / Google — the sign-in provider you choose, if any.
  • Supabase — our backend host for accounts and the leaderboard.
  • An email service — used to send the newsletter, only if you opt in.

Children's Privacy

Flappy Diver is a family-friendly game and can be played with no account and no data collection. Account features (which involve an email address) are intended for players old enough to have an Apple or Google account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.

Summary

In short: You can play Flappy Diver with no account and no data collection. If you join the global leaderboard or sign in, we collect your account identifier, email, diver name, scores, and country to run those features. We email you news only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe or delete your account at any time. We show no ads, do no tracking, and never sell your data.
